# Changelog

All notable changes to Previewer are listed here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

## [1.2.1] - 2026-10-31

### Changed

- New icon: a phone with a gradient screen inside camera-viewfinder corners.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-31

### Changed

- The toolbar shows each button's shortcut: ⌘1–⌘4 on the devices, ⌘S on Screenshot, ⌘C on copy and Esc on close. The shortcut line under the phone is gone, so the phone gets a little more room.
- iPhone SE is black (Midnight) instead of white.
- iPad Pro 11″ is shown in landscape, 1210 × 834.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-30

### Added

- The mouse pointer is a fingertip inside the phone, like DevTools' touch pointer, and darkens while you press.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-30

### Added

- One click (or Alt+Shift+P) shows the current page in a device frame on top of the tab; click again or press Esc to close.
- Four devices: iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone SE, Pixel 11 and iPad Pro 11″. Switch with ⌘1–⌘4 / Ctrl+1–4 without losing your place.
- Sends the device's User-Agent and client hints, and shows sites that normally refuse to be framed.
- Screenshot as a transparent PNG: ⌘S / Ctrl+S saves to Downloads, ⌘C / Ctrl+C copies to the clipboard.
- Settings for status bar, frame in screenshot and shadow in screenshot.
- English and Norwegian (bokmål) interface.
