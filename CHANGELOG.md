## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-30

### Added

- One click (or Alt+Shift+P) shows the current page in a device frame on top of the tab; click again or press Esc to close.
- Four devices: iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone SE, Pixel 11 and iPad Pro 11″. Switch with ⌘1–⌘4 / Ctrl+1–4 without losing your place.
- Sends the device's User-Agent and client hints, and shows sites that normally refuse to be framed.
- Screenshot as a transparent PNG: ⌘S / Ctrl+S saves to Downloads, ⌘C / Ctrl+C copies to the clipboard.
- Settings for status bar, frame in screenshot and shadow in screenshot.
- English and Norwegian (bokmål) interface.
